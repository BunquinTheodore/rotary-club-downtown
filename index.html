WHO WE ARE

Neighbors who show up

We are Rotarians of Batangas City who believe that lasting change starts at home. Together with the global family of Rotary, we turn shared concern into action, from feeding programs and medical missions to tree planting and livelihood gardens.

Guided by the motto Service Above Self, the club has served the city for more than four decades.
